## Three ideas to keep

1. **A point is a lot of money.** On a big loan paid over 30 years, one percentage point changes the payment by hundreds of dollars a month.
2. **The mortgage rate follows the 10-year Treasury yield,** what the government pays to borrow for ten years, not the Fed's rate.
3. **The same payment buys less house at a higher rate.**
