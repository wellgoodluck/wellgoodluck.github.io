## What this series is

These pages cover the ground before the [Money Traps](money-traps.html): what happens to a paycheck, where the money sits, and how to plan it. Each one is short and works the same way:

- **The short version**, in a few sentences.
- **The idea in one sentence.**
- **A worked example in dollars**, computed by the site from assumptions stated on the page.
- **What to do with it.**

Each page also has a picture drawn for the site. The pay stubs, balances and budgets in them are made up.

## The five pages

- [Where Did the Rest of It Go?](your-first-paycheck.html): what comes out of a paycheck, how the W-4 sets your withholding, and why a summer job can mean a refund.
- [The Balance Your App Shows](how-a-checking-account-works.html): pending charges, holds, deposits that haven't cleared and checks that haven't been cashed, and how to keep your own count.
- [A First Budget That Survives the Month](a-first-budget.html): fixed, flexible and once-a-year costs, the 50/30/20 rule and its critics, and what households actually spend.
- [The 0.38% Account](how-saving-works.html): interest and APY, what federal insurance covers, and why the rate matters.
- [Interest on Interest](compound-interest.html): how compounding grows savings and debt alike, and the rule of 72.

## Three more basics, in Money Traps
