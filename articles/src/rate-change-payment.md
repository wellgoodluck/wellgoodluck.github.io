## How it's worked out

The loan is the home price less the down payment. The monthly payment is the standard fixed payment that pays a loan off in equal installments over its length, with interest figured monthly at one-twelfth of the yearly rate. Total interest is all the payments added up, less the amount borrowed. "The same payment would carry" runs the arithmetic backward: the size of loan that payment pays off at the other rate.

Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. A link carries your numbers only if you copy one with them.
