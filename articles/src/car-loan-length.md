## How it's worked out

The amount borrowed is the price less the down payment and trade-in. The monthly payment is the standard fixed payment that pays a loan off in equal installments at the rate shown, with interest figured monthly at one-twelfth of the yearly rate (APR). Total interest is all the payments added up, less the amount borrowed. "Still owed" is the balance left after 60 payments.

It leaves out fees and add-ons a dealer may roll into the loan. If you plan to finance sales tax or fees, add them to the price.

Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. A link carries your numbers only if you copy one with them.
