## Prices and pay

<ul class="tools-list">
<li><a href="/tools/kept-up.html">Has It Kept Up With Prices?</a><span class="th">Your pay, a benefit, your rent or any amount, then and now, against what prices did.</span></li>
<li><a href="/tools/my-inflation-rate.html">What's My Own Inflation Rate?</a><span class="th">What you spend each month, line by line: how much your own prices rose over the past year, next to the figure for prices overall.</span></li>
<li><a href="/tools/take-home-per-hour.html">What Do I Take Home per Hour?</a><span class="th">Your take-home pay, hours and commute: what you keep for each hour given to work, and what something costs in hours of it.</span></li>
</ul>

## Borrowing

<ul class="tools-list">
<li><a href="/tools/card-payoff.html">How Long Will It Take to Pay Off a Card?</a><span class="th">Your balance, rate and monthly payment: how long payoff takes and what it costs, against paying only the minimum.</span></li>
<li><a href="/tools/car-loan-length.html">What a Car Loan Costs, by Length</a><span class="th">Your price and rate on four loan lengths: the payment, the interest and what you'd still owe.</span></li>
<li><a href="/tools/payment-plan-cost.html">What a Payment Plan Really Costs</a><span class="th">Rent-to-own, a payday loan, pay in 4 or any plan of equal payments: the total and the yearly rate it works out to.</span></li>
</ul>

## A home

<ul class="tools-list">
<li><a href="/tools/rate-change-payment.html">What a Rate Change Does to a Payment</a><span class="th">Your home price, down payment and rate: the monthly principal and interest, and what a point up or down does to it.</span></li>
<li><a href="/articles/how-a-property-tax-rate-is-set.html#try-it">Your Property Tax Bill and the Rollback Rate</a><span class="th">Your bill at the rollback rate and at last year's rates, from your assessment notice and last year's bill.</span></li>
</ul>

<ul class="tools-list">

<li><a href="/tools/car-loan-length.html">What a Car Loan Costs, by Length</a><span class="th">Your price and rate on four loan lengths: the payment, the interest and what you'd still owe.</span></li>
</ul>

## Saving

<ul class="tools-list">
