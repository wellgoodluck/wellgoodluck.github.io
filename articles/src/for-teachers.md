## What's here

Five lessons, each one class period, each built on a chart from the almanac. The current readings in every lesson update themselves when new figures come out, so a lesson always uses the latest numbers. The worked examples use fixed past figures, so their answers don't change. Each lesson has a teacher page (the plan, discussion questions and an answer key) and a printable student handout with no answers on it.

Everything is free to use, print, copy and share with credit under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and the tools save and send nothing a student types.

## Standards

Each lesson names its standard in the [Council for Economic Education's National Content Standards in K–12 Economics, 3rd edition (2025)](https://www.councilforeconed.org/wp-content/uploads/National-Content-Standards-in-K–12-Economics-3rd-Edition.pdf). State standards differ, and the lessons aren't mapped to them.
