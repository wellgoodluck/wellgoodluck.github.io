**Who calls a recession?** A committee at the National Bureau of Economic Research, usually months after it starts. "Two quarters of falling GDP" is a rule of thumb, not the official test.

**How to count on a chart:** gray bands are recessions. A **hit** is a signal that comes on within about two years before a band starts, or a few months after. A **false alarm** is a signal with no band following.
