## What this series is

Most money trouble doesn't start with one big decision. It starts with small charges, easy payments and default settings that cost more than they look, and they tend to catch people who are new to handling their own money. Each page here takes one of them and works through it the same way:

- **The short version**, in a few sentences.
- **The trap in one sentence.**
- **A worked example in dollars**, computed by the site from assumptions stated on the page.
- **How to avoid it.**
- **The bigger picture**: the rules in Washington and the argument over them, with each side's own case, dated, because these rules keep changing.

Each page also has a picture drawn for the site. The statements, pay stubs and posts in them are made up, and say so on their face.
