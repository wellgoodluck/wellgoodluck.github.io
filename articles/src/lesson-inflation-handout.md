## Three ideas to keep

1. **Inflation is a speed, not a level.** When inflation falls, prices are still rising, just more slowly. Prices rarely go back down.
2. **One number needs context.** Compare it with last month ({{v cpi.previous}}, {{v cpi.previous period}}), a year ago ({{v cpi.year_ago}}) and the years before the pandemic ({{v cpi.baseline}} on average, 2015–19).
3. **"Real" means after inflation.** A raise smaller than inflation buys less, even though the paycheck is bigger.

## Words

- **Consumer Price Index (CPI):** the government's measure of what households pay for a fixed basket of goods and services.
- **Year over year:** compared with the same month a year earlier.
- **Median:** the middle value; half of all months were higher, half lower.
- **Nominal:** in the dollars on the price tag or paycheck.
- **Real:** adjusted for inflation, so it measures what money can buy.
