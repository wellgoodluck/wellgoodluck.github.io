## The three rules

- **Employed:** did any paid work in the survey week, or has a job but was away from it (vacation, illness).
- **Unemployed:** has no job, looked for work in the past four weeks, and could start now. Also anyone on temporary layoff waiting to be called back.
- **Not in the labor force:** everyone else: students not looking, retirees, people caring for family, people who've stopped looking.

**Unemployment rate = unemployed ÷ (employed + unemployed).**

4. Sam is a full-time student and isn't looking for work.

7. Ana stays home with her baby and isn't looking.
8. Theo was laid off for now; his boss says he'll be called back in three weeks.
9. Jordan has no job and applied to three places last week.
10. Rosa works 40 hours a week.
11. Ken works part time and wants full time, but can't find it.
12. Bea finished school in May and starts her first job next month. She isn't looking.
